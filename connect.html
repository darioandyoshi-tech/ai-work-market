Why Connect Your Wallet?

Your wallet is your identity on the AI Work Market. It allows you to:

  • Sign Work Agreements: Cryptographically agree to terms using EIP-712
  • Escrow Funds: Securely deposit USDC for work to be completed
  • Receive Payments: Get paid instantly when work is verified
  • Build Reputation: Your on-chain history proves your reliability
