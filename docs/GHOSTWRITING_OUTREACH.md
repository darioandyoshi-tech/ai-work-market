# AI Ghostwriting Agency — Cold outreach templates

3 channels: X (Twitter) DMs, LinkedIn DMs, niche newsletter replies. Each is a template — copy and personalize the opening sentence. The point is volume: 20-30 DMs/day, 5-10 newsletter replies/week.

## Channel 1: X (Twitter) DM (target: founders with 1K-100K followers)

```
hey [first name] — saw your post on [specific topic]. real question:
do you have a system for your content or is it whenever-you-feel-like-it?

i run a small AI ghostwriting agency. 12 clients. the agent scrapes
their past posts / podcasts / interviews, builds a voice profile, and
delivers a 7-day calendar every Sunday in their actual voice.

clients spend 15 min/week reviewing. the agent does 90% of the work.
the bottleneck is fit, not production.

$1,500/month. 4-6 month minimum commitment. 3 spots open.

if you want, i can build a free voice profile + sample calendar for
your actual posts (no charge, no signup). takes 60 seconds:

https://ai-work-market.ai/ghostwriting

either way, no hard pitch — figured i'd reach out since your content
is good. the cadence is the only thing between you and the next level.
```

## Targeting (how to find the 30 DMs/day)

### X (Twitter) search terms
- "I should post more" + "AI" — founders admitting the bottleneck
- "Building in public" + bio with 1K-100K followers
- Reply guys on crypto/AI tweets (people who post long, thoughtful replies)

### LinkedIn search
- Title: "Founder" OR "CEO" OR "VP" + Industry: "Crypto" OR "AI"
- 1K-50K followers (filter by follower count)
- Posted in last 30 days (active users)

### Newsletter / community targets
- Lenny's Newsletter, The Generalist, AI Tidbits, Ben Thompson
- Indie Hackers, AI agent Discords
- Substack in the agent/crypto/AI space

## Volume targets
- Day 1-2: 20 DMs, expect 1-2 replies
- Day 3-5: 30 DMs/day, expect 3-5 replies
- Day 7-14: 30 DMs/day, expect 1-3 booked calls/week
- Day 14-30: 5-10 booked calls → 1-3 clients

## Conversion math
- 30 DMs/day × 22 working days = 660 DMs in 30 days
- ~3% reply rate = 20 replies
- ~30% of replies book a call = 6 calls
- ~50% of calls close = 3 clients
- 3 clients × $1,500/month = $4,500/month recurring

**Note:** the math above is the Indie Hackers case study median. Real operators see 1-3 clients in the first 30 days. The variance is huge — the only way to find out is to do the outreach.

## What NOT to do
- ❌ Pitch the technology (clients don't care about GPT-4 or voice profiles)
- ❌ Send a generic "i can help you with content" message
- ❌ Lead with "I'm from [Company] and we do..."
- ❌ Promise "viral" or "guaranteed growth"
- ❌ Use the word "synergy" or any corporate jargon

## What TO do
- ✅ Reference a specific post they made
- ✅ Ask a question (real curiosity, not manipulation)
- ✅ Lead with the demo (free value, no charge)
- ✅ Make the next step small (reply with a time, not a contract)
- ✅ Follow up once after 3-4 days if no reply
