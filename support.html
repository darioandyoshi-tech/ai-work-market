Support.

Help for paid products, escrow disputes, stuck proofs, and mistaken payments.

How to reach us

Open an issue on our GitHub repo — that is the fastest, most reliable channel and it keeps a public record:

github.com/darioandyoshi-tech/ai-work-market/issues

For anything sensitive (private keys, suspected compromise, fund-loss reports), do not post details publicly. Open a GitHub issue marked [SECURITY] with only the affected address/tx hash, and we will follow up privately.

Expected response times

Paid products / fulfillment within 2 business days

Questions about a product you purchased, delivery status, or a missing fulfillment link.

Escrow disputes acknowledge 2 business days · resolve 7 business days

Disputes are resolved by the platform multisig under our published Trust Policy and Dispute Policy. Include the intent id, tx hash, and any evidence (work spec, proof URI, communication).

Stuck proofs / stuck intents within 2 business days

An intent stuck in Funded past the work deadline, or ProofSubmitted past the review deadline. Include the intent id and tx hash.

Mistaken payments within 2 business days

Sent USDC to the wrong address or wrong amount. Include the tx hash and the intended intent id. Note: on-chain payments are irreversible; recovery depends on the escrow state and dispute policy.

Before you open a ticket

  • Check Trust Policy for escrow, refund, and dispute rules.
  • Check Documentation and the API docs for self-serve answers.
  • Have your intent id, tx hash, and product slug ready.

Security & fund-loss reports

If you believe funds are at risk or a vulnerability exists, open a [SECURITY] issue with only the affected address/tx hash. Do not include private keys or full credentials anywhere in public channels.

Response target for security reports: within 24 hours.
